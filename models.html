Methods & Model Architecture Framework

Mathematical Formulations, 3-Elo Complete Dynamics, Model M32, and Parameter Specifications

This section details the mathematical formulations and parameter estimates for the 3-Elo Complete rating architecture and the \(M_{32}\) Bivariate Poisson GLM forecasting model.

1. The 3-Elo Complete Rating Architecture (3eloC)

The 3-Elo Complete system decomposes national team strength into three dynamic state variables evaluated chronologically across all match dates: 1. Overall Rating (\(R^e\)): Aggregate winning capacity. 2. Offensive Rating (\(R^o\)): Goal-scoring potency. 3. Defensive Rating (\(R^d\)): Defensive resistance.

Mathematical Rating Update Equations

Following match \(k\) between Home Team \(H\) and Away Team \(A\) with match goal outcome \((g_H, g_A)\):

1. Overall Rating Update (\(R^e\)):

Expected win probability \(E_H\) is computed via logistic sigmoid: \[E_H = \frac{1}{1 + 10^{-\frac{(R^e_H + H_{\text{overall}} - R^e_A)}{D_{\text{overall}}}}}\]

Goal difference margin scaling multiplier \(G(\Delta g)\) (where \(\Delta g = |g_H - g_A|\)): \[G(\Delta g) = \begin{cases} 1.0 & \text{if } \Delta g \le 1 \\ G_2 & \text{if } \Delta g = 2 \\ \frac{a_{\text{margin}} + \Delta g}{b_{\text{margin}}} & \text{if } \Delta g \ge 3 \end{cases}\]

Effective overall \(K\)-factor scaling: \[K_{\text{eff}} = K_{\text{base}} \cdot \left(M_{\text{overall}}\right)^{I_{\text{comp}}} \cdot G(\Delta g)\]

Overall rating update: \[R^e_{H, k+1} = R^e_{H, k} + K_{\text{eff}} \cdot (S_H - E_H)\] \[R^e_{A, k+1} = R^e_{A, k} + K_{\text{eff}} \cdot (S_A - E_A)\]

2. Tactical Style Updates (Offensive \(R^o\) & Defensive \(R^d\)):

Expected goal intensities (\(\lambda_H, \lambda_A\)): \[\lambda_H = \mu_{\text{base}} \cdot 10^{\frac{(R^o_H - R^d_A + H_{\text{style}})}{D_{\text{style}}}}\] \[\lambda_A = \mu_{\text{base}} \cdot 10^{\frac{(R^o_A - R^d_H - H_{\text{style}})}{D_{\text{style}}}}\]

Effective style \(K\)-factor: \[K_{\text{style}} = K_{\text{base}} \cdot K_{\text{scale}} \cdot \left(M_{\text{style}}\right)^{I_{\text{comp}}}\]

Tactical style updates: \[R^o_{H, k+1} = R^o_{H, k} + K_{\text{style}} \cdot (g_H - \lambda_H)\] \[R^d_{A, k+1} = R^d_{A, k} + K_{\text{style}} \cdot (\lambda_H - g_H)\] \[R^o_{A, k+1} = R^o_{A, k} + K_{\text{style}} \cdot (g_A - \lambda_A)\] \[R^d_{H, k+1} = R^d_{H, k} + K_{\text{style}} \cdot (\lambda_A - g_A)\]

2. Model \(M_{32}\) Bivariate Poisson GLM Framework (B-I-Q-T-C)

Model \(M_{32}\) is a 21-parameter Bivariate Poisson Generalized Linear Model (GLM) with quadratic rating response, non-linear temporal trends, competition weighting, and separate Home/Away feature vectors.

Log-Linear Poisson Intensities (\(\lambda_H, \lambda_A\)):

The expected goal intensities for the Home team (\(\lambda_H\)) and Away team (\(\lambda_A\)) are modeled via exponential link functions:

\[\ln \lambda_H = \boldsymbol{\beta}_{\text{home}}^T \boldsymbol{x}_H\]

\[\ln \lambda_A = \boldsymbol{\beta}_{\text{away}}^T \boldsymbol{x}_A\]

Feature Vectors (\(\boldsymbol{x}_H\) and \(\boldsymbol{x}_A\)):

The 9-element feature vectors incorporate overall strength differentials, tactical style interactions, non-linear quadratic terms, venue indicators, and temporal trend polynomials:

\[\boldsymbol{x}_H = \big[ 1, \, \Delta R_{e, H}, \, \Delta R_{\text{style}, H}, \, \Delta R_{e, H}^2, \, \Delta R_{\text{style}, H}^2, \, I_{\text{home}}, \, t_{\text{norm}}, \, t_{\text{norm}}^2, \, I_{\text{comp}} \big]^T\]

\[\boldsymbol{x}_A = \big[ 1, \, \Delta R_{e, A}, \, \Delta R_{\text{style}, A}, \, \Delta R_{e, A}^2, \, \Delta R_{\text{style}, A}^2, \, I_{\text{home}}, \, t_{\text{norm}}, \, t_{\text{norm}}^2, \, I_{\text{comp}} \big]^T\]

where: - \(\Delta R_{e, H} = R^e_H - R^e_A\) (Overall Elo Differential) - \(\Delta R_{\text{style}, H} = R^o_H - R^d_A\) (Home Offense vs Away Defense Differential) - \(\Delta R_{\text{style}, A} = R^o_A - R^d_H\) (Away Offense vs Home Defense Differential) - \(I_{\text{home}} \in \{0, 1\}\) (Home Venue Advantage Indicator) - \(t_{\text{norm}} = \frac{\text{Year} - 1872}{154}\) (Normalized Historical Time Trend) - \(I_{\text{comp}} \in \{0, 1\}\) (Competitive Tournament Match Indicator)

Bivariate Dixon-Coles Goal Probability Distribution:

The joint scoreline probability matrix \(P(G_H = x, G_A = y)\) applies the Dixon-Coles low-score dependency factor \(\rho = -0.082093\):

\[P(G_H = x, G_A = y) = \tau(x, y) \cdot \left( \frac{\lambda_H^x e^{-\lambda_H}}{x!} \right) \cdot \left( \frac{\lambda_A^y e^{-\lambda_A}}{y!} \right)\]

where:

\[\tau(x, y) = \begin{cases} 1 - \lambda_H \lambda_A \rho & \text{if } x = 0, y = 0 \\ 1 + \lambda_H \rho & \text{if } x = 1, y = 0 \\ 1 + \lambda_A \rho & \text{if } x = 0, y = 1 \\ 1 - \rho & \text{if } x = 1, y = 1 \\ 1 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}\]
